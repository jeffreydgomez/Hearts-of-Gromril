### Base mechanics to account for when simplifying

- **Ship modules:** now base content through Man the Guns integration. A simpler navy would need changes to equipment, technologies, variants and AI production together.

The publisher's October 2026 energy discussion also describes further work, so reassess the exact installed version before designing a replacement. Our current mod retains the installed industrial-power mechanic with **Warpstone as the energy source** and **Horses in the former coal resource slot**. [Publisher energy discussion](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=394360&feed=steam_community_announcements).

## Notes

The global flag catalogue is at the top of [End times lore reference](End%20times%20lore%20reference.md#proposed-global-flags-the-shared-story-state). In the tables below, a flag written as `crisis_started` means the proposed exact key **`wet_story_crisis_started`**. All of those flags are designs, not implemented script. **H** means a route following the narrative's motives and broad sequence; **A** means an explicitly ahistorical alternative. Historical choices still permit a successful defence or rescue: do not force a loss merely to reproduce the source.

Use **focuses** for strategic commitments, **decisions** for choices and spending, **missions** for interruptible objectives with deadlines, and **events** for consequences and diplomacy. Keep economic/military foundations available on both routes so an early failed mission cannot make a country unplayable. Costs, durations and bonuses below are qualitative concepts, not approved balance values.

### Shared design contract

- The common opening should take the nation from its actual 2519 setup into the crisis. No free canonical territory transfers, pre-completed 2520s events or retroactive source-history updates.
- The original basic template used **Internal**, **External**, and **Production** with a common trunk. Country-specific user instructions take precedence: **Reikland revision 2 has four independent roots and no shared opening**. Show prerequisites and consequences before permanent choices; preserve recovery/survival routes.
- Use current control, surviving characters, custody and resources alongside the global flags. A prior victory flag alone never proves a country still owns a fortress or holds an artifact.
- Generic Order faction creation/joining requires **100% tension**; creation also needs a curated major and no existing Order-led faction. **Reikland's confirmed focus programme is an explicit 25% exception** for formation/recruitment; an ally already fighting the Everchosen still requires 100%. Preserve the creator roster and consent to join an existing faction.
- Shared events have one resolver. Elven settlements, Nagash's resurrection, Eight Peaks and the final ritual cannot give incompatible rewards to different trees. Resolve simultaneous player choices through an explicit diplomatic response or contest, not by whichever country's daily event happens to run first.
- If an ally disappears or a target has already fallen, offer a bypass with an appropriate reduced/changed reward, a recovery mission or a clearly explained failure. Do not bypass a failed prerequisite and still award the historical outcome.
- Keep a small set of non-tree global crisis events for actors outside this first batch. Full Sylvania/Nagash, Athel Loren, Hexoatl and Grimgor trees are strong candidates for the next batch. Their actions remain real dependencies, not free achievements credited to one of these ten nations.
